# Brand Icons

These icons are vendored so the setup wizard can render reliably without network access.

- Audacity, Blender, Godot Engine, GitHub, LMMS, NVIDIA, Ollama, Unity, and Unreal Engine icons are sourced from Simple Icons: https://simpleicons.org/
- ComfyUI and OpenClaw icons are sourced from homarr-labs/dashboard-icons: https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons

Brand marks remain trademarks of their respective owners and are used here only to identify the software options shown in the installer.
